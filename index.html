Exam day

The final is 100 points (8.33% of CH 102). You may bring one letter-size sheet.

Your exam sheet

Side 1 has every equation, worked example and key result, so it works even if only one side is allowed. Side 2 has observations, rules, tables and safety. Print at 100% ("Actual size"), two-sided, flip on long edge.

The night before

    In the exam

    First 2 minSkim the whole exam. Do the definition and safety questions first: they're fast points.
    CalculationsWrite the equation from your sheet, plug in with units, check mL → L or use mmol/mL, then sig figs.
    Stuck?Ask "which lab is this from?" and find that lab's box on your sheet.
    Last 5 minCheck signs (ΔT, E_cell), mole ratios (Ca(OH)₂ is 2:1), and that every answer has units.
